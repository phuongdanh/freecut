Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapFree ("CapFree", "we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our web-based video editing software. CapFree is designed to run locally in your browser and keep your media on your device whenever possible.

1) Information we process

Depending on how you use CapFree, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Project and editing data: timeline edits, effects settings, captions, markers, and other project metadata.
  • Local media references: selected file names, metadata, and file handles needed to relink media in your projects.
  • Local caches and generated assets: thumbnails, waveform data, and proxy data created to improve editing performance.
  • Basic technical data: browser/environment information and diagnostics generated when you use optional debug features or contact support.

2) Local-first processing

CapFree is built for local browser processing. Your imported media files are intended to stay on your device and are processed in-browser using technologies such as WebGPU, WebCodecs, IndexedDB, and OPFS.

In normal usage, we do not require you to upload your source media to our servers to edit or export your videos.

3) Why we use data

  • Provide core editing features (timeline, effects, export, and project management).
  • Save and restore projects locally in your browser.
  • Improve app stability, performance, and compatibility across supported browsers.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported bugs.

4) Storage and retention

CapFree stores project-related data in your browser storage (for example, IndexedDB and OPFS). This data remains on your device until you delete it, clear browser storage, or uninstall browser/site data.

Retention duration may vary by browser settings, storage pressure, private/incognito mode behavior, and your own cleanup actions.

5) Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information only when reasonably necessary to: (a) comply with legal obligations, (b) protect our rights and users, or (c) investigate abuse, security incidents, or fraud.

6) International users

If you access CapFree from outside the jurisdiction where it is operated, you understand that information you choose to provide (for example, support emails) may be processed in other countries in accordance with applicable laws.

7) Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining security of your devices and browser environment.

8) Children's privacy

CapFree is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

9) Your choices and rights

  • You can delete local project data from within the app or by clearing browser storage.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, where applicable.

10) Third-party services

CapFree may link to third-party websites, libraries, or services. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

11) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date.
